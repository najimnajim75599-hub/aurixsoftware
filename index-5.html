Everything in one place

Six sections. One connected workspace.

Each part of AURIX does one job well, and they all talk to each other.

Memory

Save notes and ideas, add tags, mark what matters and search everything instantly.

Find

Search files you choose by name, type, tag or your own description. Honest keyword search, not fake AI.

Flow

Turn ideas into projects with tasks, deadlines, progress and attached notes and files.

Connect

See how files, projects, notes, ideas and tasks relate in an interactive knowledge graph.

Activity

A clean timeline of what changed and when. You control what is recorded.

Private by design

Privacy

Private by design

All your data stays on your computer in a local database. AURIX never uploads files, never sends notes to online AI services, never scans your computer without permission, and only indexes folders you choose. You can pause indexing, remove folders and delete everything from Settings.

Is it really free?

Yes, the core software is free to use.

How do I back up my data?

Settings → Backup and restore → Create backup. You get one file to keep on a USB drive.
